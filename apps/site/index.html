Luna · default

Your day at a glance

Open a small calendar whenever you need it. Select a day to see your events, notes, and todos together, with the moon phase close at hand. Colored dots make busy days easy to spot.

Sol

More room to explore

Browse a larger calendar with lunar dates, solar terms, and week numbers. Check the moon phase or jot down a note for any day. Click the heading to jump between months and years.

Features

Features at a glance

Keep track of dates, plans, and the little things you want to remember.

Lunar date & 24 solar terms

Lunar dates and 24 solar terms, computed on-device. New botanical line-art icons use three gentle colors, with compact artwork on Mac and detailed icons in the browser. Icons follow your lunar-date setting.

System calendar

See events from your Mac calendars without changing them. Colored dots mark busy days; select a date to see its schedule and links to video meetings.

Holidays

Subscribe to holidays for Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, and Thailand, or add your own iCal feeds. Mainland China includes adjusted workdays, clearly marked even when lunar dates are hidden. Each subscription has its own color.

Moon phases

See the moon phase for any date. In Luna, today's circle follows the moon, with warm yellow tones in light mode. The optional 3D Moon uses your location on-device — location is never uploaded.

Diary & todos

Free-text diary entries per day on macOS; type [] buy milk (or [ ] / [x]) for todos with checkboxes — completed items strike through. Export as JSON.

Date marks & diary

Color marks and diary entries on individual days in the browser extension.

Menu Bar & languages

Customizable status bar items: moon icon, date badge, day number, lunar day, weekday, and time — reorder as you like. Interface languages: 简体中文, 繁體中文, English, ไทย (Thai shows the Buddhist Era).
