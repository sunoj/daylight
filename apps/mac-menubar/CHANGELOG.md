# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- Permission requests, including the system prompts, now appear in the app's language: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or Thai.
- The screens before the calendar and location requests explain what the access is for and continue with a single Continue button.
- On first launch in English or Thai, the menu bar shows the date instead of the lunar day, and lunar dates start off. Chinese users see no change.
- 12/24-hour time and the first day of the week now follow your region on first launch. Saturday is available as a week start.
- Settings links to the privacy policy and to help, and scrolls on smaller displays so Quit is always reachable.
- The Taiwan and Thailand holiday presets were removed. An existing Thailand subscription keeps working as a custom calendar link.

### Fixed
- Choosing "Not now" before the 3D moon no longer brings up the location prompt anyway.
- The moon phase in the calendar and 3D view was about a day and a half behind the sky. It now matches the menu bar icon.
- Holiday calendars now refresh daily or weekly, as the "Auto update" setting always said.
- Mainland China and Hong Kong holiday names appear in English and Thai.
- The menu bar time and date update on the minute and after sleep. The calendar refreshes "today" each time it opens.
- Copy, paste, select all and undo work in text fields, and Command-Q quits.
- With location turned off, the 3D moon offers a link to System Settings instead of showing a position for 0°, 0°.
- "Add events only" calendar access now offers System Settings instead of an empty agenda.
- VoiceOver reads names for icon buttons and the menu bar item instead of symbol names.
- Traditional Chinese no longer shows a few Simplified characters.

## [2.0.5] - 2026-09-28

### Improved
- The calendar opens faster on the first click after launch, and moving between months is smoother.

### Fixed
- A long diary note no longer widens the calendar. It wraps onto several lines.
- Days with many diary notes now scroll instead of overlapping.
- The moon phase line and location messages now appear in the selected language.
- Settings shows the app's current version.

## [2.0.4] - 2026-09-26

### Fixed
- Fixed a crash when opening the calendar from the menu bar on a fresh install. Bundled fonts and the 3D moon model now load from inside the app.

## [2.0.3] - 2026-09-24

### Changed
- Removed the retired remote default-configuration request. Holiday subscriptions continue to refresh from their selected sources.

## [2.0.2] - 2026-09-24

### Added
- Traditional Chinese interface for calendars, moon phases, holiday labels, settings, and daily notes. The language can also follow a Traditional Chinese system locale.

## [2.0.1] - 2026-09-24

### Improved
- Redrawn botanical artwork for all 24 solar terms, with compact icons beside the moon phase. Solar-term icons follow the lunar-date setting.
- A soft background distinguishes the selected date. In Luna, today's circle follows the moon phase with warm lunar colors in light mode.
- Mainland China holiday subscriptions include adjusted workdays with a separate workday badge. Refresh existing subscriptions once to import the new dates.
- Improved English labels in the compact calendar, day details, and diary controls.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-08-28

### Added
- Luna and Sol menu bar calendar layouts, lunar dates, solar terms, and moon views.
- Read-only system calendar access, holiday subscriptions, diary entries, and todos.
- Signed and notarized direct downloads with automatic updates.
