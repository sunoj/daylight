# Changelog

## [2.0.3] - 2026-09-24

### Changed
- Removed the retired remote default-configuration request. Holiday subscriptions continue to refresh from their selected sources.

## [2.0.2] - 2026-09-24

### Added
- Traditional Chinese interface for calendars, moon phases, holiday labels, settings, and daily notes. The language can also follow a Traditional Chinese system locale.

## [2.0.1] - 2026-09-24

### Improved
- Redrawn botanical artwork for all 24 solar terms, with compact icons beside the moon phase. Solar-term icons follow the lunar-date setting.
- A soft background distinguishes the selected date. In Luna, today's circle follows the moon phase with warm lunar colors in light mode.
- Mainland China holiday subscriptions include adjusted workdays with a separate workday badge. Refresh existing subscriptions once to import the new dates.
- Improved English labels in the compact calendar, day details, and diary controls.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-08-28

### Added
- Luna and Sol menu bar calendar layouts, lunar dates, solar terms, and moon views.
- Read-only system calendar access, holiday subscriptions, diary entries, and todos.
- Signed and notarized direct downloads with automatic updates.
